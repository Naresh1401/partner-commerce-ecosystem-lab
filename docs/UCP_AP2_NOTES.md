# UCP And AP2 Revision Notes

UCP is useful to understand as a commerce interoperability idea: agents, merchants, and payment providers need a common language across discovery, checkout, and post-purchase support.

AP2 is useful to understand as a payment authorization idea: agent-performed purchases need verifiable evidence that a human or trusted surface authorized the purchase.

What I should remember:

- Keep product and checkout objects structured.
- Bind payment authorization to a specific checkout.
- Verify risky claims in deterministic code.
- Keep receipts and mandate IDs for dispute evidence.
- Treat the agent as an untrusted caller unless proven otherwise.
